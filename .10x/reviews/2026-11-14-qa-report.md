# Quality review: the ten parity amendments (PA-01 to PA-10)

Feature `glass-demo-amendments`, branch `claude/jolly-johnson-gd61vv`. Reviewed read-only on
2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15, by reading code, migrations and tests. No test, build or type check was
run (the machine was shared with a long test run), so "confirmed" below always means "traced in the
code at the lines given", never "seen failing".

The branch moved during the review. Most of the reading was done at commit `9080d9fa`; the head
was `a4fb2e37` at the end (two commits later: catalog's class names on the public schedule, and a
state note). Somebody else was changing the working tree at the end: two controller files
(finding B-1) and `enrollment/__tests__/registration-waivers.spec.ts` had uncommitted changes,
which were not reviewed. Line numbers are those of `a4fb2e37`.

## 1. Summary and verdict

**Verdict: not ready to be called complete. One merge blocker, eight major findings.** Six of the
eight majors are in what the six tracks did not build together: what a tier, a day selection, an
add-on or a uniform means for the waiting list, a transfer, the arrival desk, the family calendar
and a team that waits. The other two are a missing notice (a place offer nobody is told about) and
what the public catalog keeps saying after a pricing switch is turned off.

What is sound: each feature taken alone is implemented to the repository's rules. Every command
checks its capability inside its unit of work; every new table has organization scoping, composite
keys and database guards; almost every invariant has a test that inserts the violating row and
names the constraint that refused it; the three gap journeys keep today's behaviour with the switch
off and gain the new behaviour with it on. PA-05, PA-06, PA-08, PA-09 and PA-10 have no finding
above Minor in their own module, apart from M-7.

Recommendation: fix B-1 before merging. `price_tiers_addons` and `uniforms` should not be turned
on for an organization (the demo included, where they are on) until M-1 to M-4 are fixed or the
affected paths are refused; M-5 to M-8 before `team_waitlist`, `member_pricing` and
`per_session_pricing` are turned on for a real organization.

## 3. Findings

### Blocker

**B-1. Six write routes of track D are not classified for record authority at the committed head.**
Confirmed by reading; not run.

- Where: `apps/api/src/modules/catalog/controllers/offering-extras.controller.ts` (four `@Post`
  handlers) and `apps/api/src/modules/enrollment/controllers/uniform-orders.controller.ts` (two)
  carry neither `@RecordAuthority` nor `@NoRecordAuthority` at `a4fb2e37`.
- Why it matters: `apps/api/src/modules/migration/services/authority-routes.ts:28` states that
  `__tests__/authority.spec.ts` (`classifyWriteRoutes`) fails on an unclassified write route, so
  `pnpm verify` should be red at this commit. Track D did not run the migration module's tests.
- State: an uncommitted change adding the decorators to both files was in the working tree at the
  end of this review. It was not reviewed.
- Fix: commit it, then run `modules/migration/__tests__/authority.spec.ts` and
  `route-classification.spec.ts`. Check the category chosen for a hand-over.

### Major

**M-1. A day selection is honoured by the price, the session pools and the attendance list, and by
nothing else.** Confirmed by reading; no test.

- Arrival: `entry/services/arrival-checks.ts:230-256` (`sessionBasis`) accepts any confirmed
  enrollment of the session's offering. `EnrollmentPort.participationsOf` does not carry
  `selected_session_ids`. A child who bought Monday is checked in on Wednesday with a confirmed
  basis and no issue.
- Family calendar: `enrollment/services/family-calendar.service.ts:100-126` lists every scheduled
  session of the offering for each active enrollment. The family of a two-day child sees five camp
  days.
- After purchase nobody can read the days: the `Enrollment` contract has neither tier nor days
  (admitted), and the order line says "Camp: Two days", not which.
- On the waiver question asked: the waiver check is per offering, so the waiver result is right on
  any day. The consequence is the wrong one upstream of it: the child is admitted on a day not
  paid for, on which the session pool does not count them and the attendance list
  (`session-roster.ts:36`) does not expect them.
- Fix: carry `selectedSessionIds` on the participation summary; in `sessionBasis` a confirmed
  enrollment whose selection excludes the session is a blocking `no_participation` (or a new
  code) with a next action; filter the family calendar and the calendar feed the same way; expose
  tier and days on `Enrollment`. Add an arrival test and a calendar test for a chosen-day child.

**M-2. The waiting list does not work for an offering sold by tier.** Confirmed by reading; no
test.

- Joining: `enrollment/services/waitlist.service.ts:432-452` looks at the offering-wide pools
  only. A chosen-day line takes no place in them (`registration-extras.ts:650`). When Monday is
  full but the offering-wide pool has room, the hold fails with CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE, the customer
  app says "You can join the waitlist for a participant"
  (`apps/mobile/src/screens/registration/BasketScreen.tsx:398-404`), and the join is refused with
  `CAPACITY_AVAILABLE`, "A place is available now; register instead of waiting." The same happens
  when the full pool is an add-on's.
- Accepting: `waitlist.service.ts:1482-1494` opens the line through
  `baskets.service.ts:1563-1619`, which stores `price_tier_id: null`. The quote then throws
  `TIER_REQUIRED` (`finance/pricing/line-pricing.service.ts:137-146`). The place is held and
  cannot be paid; the hold expires. Trial conversion uses the same function. Neither client
  mentions `TIER_REQUIRED`.
- Fix (smallest): refuse to join, and refuse to generate an offer for, an offering whose effective
  rule has tiers while the capability is on, with a reason, and say so on the customer screen.
  Proper: let a waitlist entry carry the tier and days, wait on the pools that selection needs,
  and open the accepted line with them. Add a test for each path.

**M-3. Session capacity is counted only for lines bought under a chosen-day rule.** Confirmed by
reading; partly admitted in track D (section 5).

- `registration-extras.ts:635-651` holds a session pool for each attended session only when the
  line names a tier and the rule in effect has a chosen-day tier.
  `capacity.service.ts:326-373` sizes that pool from the offering's seats, independently of what
  the offering-wide pool already holds.
- Not counted on any session pool, yet expected at every session (`session-roster.ts:36`):
  1. a participant transferred in (`enrollments.service.ts:1335-1341` and `:1595-1600` take the
     offering-wide pools only; the new enrollment has no tier);
  2. every registration made before the offering's rule gained a chosen-day tier.
- Scenario: a camp of 20 has 12 full-week registrations. Staff add a "single day" tier in a new
  rule version. Each day can now be sold 20 more times.
- Fix: when a rule version with a chosen-day tier takes effect, commit the existing confirmed
  enrollments to each session pool (or refuse such a rule while live enrollments exist); make a
  transfer into such an offering hold the session pools or refuse it. Add a database-level test.

**M-4. A required uniform is enforced only on `POST /baskets/{id}/lines`.** Confirmed by reading;
no test.

- `UNIFORM_REQUIRED` is thrown only in `registration-extras.ts:221-231` (`resolve`), called from
  `baskets.service.ts:437`. Lines opened by `openForLine` (waitlist acceptance, trial conversion)
  and enrollments created by a transfer (`enrollments.service.ts:1559`) or a staff placement never
  pass through it: the registration completes with no uniform ordered and nothing says one is
  missing.
- Fix: check the requirement where the enrollment is created (the hold), not where the line is
  added, and give the paths that cannot choose a size a refusal with a next action.

**M-5. A team that waits keeps its players' paid registrations and uniforms, and nobody is told.**
Confirmed by reading; no test.

- `competition/services/team-entries.service.ts:782-833` (`moveToWaiting`) refuses only an entry
  that has a division or a team obligation. Players who joined while the entry was `applied`
  (allowed: `enrollment/services/registration-evaluator.ts:135`) stay confirmed and charged, their
  uniform orders stay `paid`, and the uniform list of the team offers "Hand over".
- `competition.team_entry_status_changed` has no subscriber, so nothing happens either when the
  offer expires, or when the team is withdrawn or rejected from the list.
- While the team waits, a new player is refused with "The team entry is not taking players" and
  the next action "choose another team"
  (`enrollment/eligibility/registration-eligibility.ts:251-257`), which is wrong advice for a team
  that is waiting for a place.
- PA-10 says a waiting entry owes nothing; that holds for the team's obligation only.
- Fix: decide and build one of: refuse to make an entry wait while it has live player
  registrations; or raise an operational exception naming them; mark the uniform list for a team
  that is not placed; a reason code of its own for a waiting team.

## 2. The debt Track A reported: which fix was chosen

The basket price snapshot showed a per-session rule's base price as "Base price each". Of the two
ways to close it (relabel what is shown, or make the figure right), the second was chosen: the
snapshot of a line that is not a plain term price carries `estimate`, worked out by enrollment
from the same facts a quote uses and in the quote's order (tier, then member price, then
per-session multiplication), and the clients show it as an estimate: the customer basket says
"Estimated price each" and "The price is confirmed when you review it.", the staff basket line
says "Estimate: the quote decides the price". A plain term line stores exactly the snapshot it
stored before.
The cancellation and transfer previews pass the estimate where there is one. The quote remains the
only priced document; the estimate is never charged.

## 3. Deviations from the architect contract, and why

1. **A fourth uniform state, `canceled`.** The contract names ordered, paid and handed over. A
   released basket line or a canceled registration needs somewhere to go that is not a deletion, so
   `UniformOrderMachine` has `canceled` from `ordered` and `paid`.
2. **A new read, `GET /v1/offerings/{id}/purchase-options`,** instead of extending the registration
   options: those belong to files of another track (the registration evaluator).
3. **`capacity_pools`:** a column `scope_session_id` and the scope `addon`; the unique constraint of
   migration 0500 is replaced by a unique index that includes the session, and the lazy pool
   insert uses a targetless `on conflict do nothing`. The add-on's own pool needed a key the old
   constraint could not express.
4. **`quote_lines.source_id` is guarded by a trigger, not a foreign key:** it names an add-on or an
   offering uniform depending on `line_kind`. The trigger checks organization and offering.
5. **The uniqueness `(quote_id, basket_line_id)` of quote lines is widened** to one place line per
   basket line and one line per add-on or uniform (two partial unique indexes).
6. **Add-ons and uniforms carry their own charge lines; discounts never apply to them.** Nothing in
   the amendment says a discount reaches an extra, so none was assumed.
7. **Who may set add-ons and uniforms: the roles of price rules** (finance operator, general
   manager, system administrator). Hand-over uses the registration staff roles. Both are
   engineering defaults.
8. **A line that names a tier, an add-on or a uniform is not quoted once its capability is off**
   (`CAPABILITY_DISABLED`) rather than silently priced at the base price. A rule that has tiers
   prices a line without a tier at its base price while the capability is off, exactly as before.
9. **A uniform is not part of a cancellation share.** The policy share applies to the place and its
   add-ons; the uniform is named in the explanation, and a transfer of a place bought with extras,
   or into an offering sold by tier, answers `review_required`. Refund of a uniform and the meaning
   of a transfer for extras are **Pending decision** (DEC-009, DEC-101).
10. **Chosen-day tiers hold session pools.** While the effective rule has a tier of chosen days,
    every tier line holds the session pool of each session attended, and a line of chosen days
    takes no place in the offering-wide pool. This is how "capacity counts a participant only on
    selected sessions" is enforced by the database.
11. **A team season's uniform is the uniform of the league offering its teams register through.**
    Nothing is stored on the season (the competition module belongs to another track); the list by
    team reads the team entry recorded on the basket line.
12. **Migration numbers:** 0215 to 0217, 0521 to 0523, 0690 and 0691 are used; 0218, 0219, 0524 to
    0529 and 0692 to 0694 are free.
13. **Commit messages carry no attribution trailer**, following `CLAUDE.md` and the brief.

## 5. Technical debt created or left

- A line opened by another command (a waitlist acceptance, a trial conversion) names no tier; for
  an offering sold by tier its quote is refused with `TIER_REQUIRED` and staff register through a
  basket. A transfer into such an offering is reviewed by finance.
- Registrations made before an offering had tiers hold the offering-wide pool only and are not
  counted on the session pools. A session scheduled after a full-week purchase is attended, but
  its pool does not count that participant.
- A canceled session of a day selection changes nothing by itself (refund and credit policy,
  DEC-009, not built).
- The arrival checks (another track's file) do not read the day selection: a participant arriving
  on a day not chosen is checked as a participant of the offering. The attendance read does list
  only who is expected.
- An add-on's capacity cannot be edited (retire it and create it again). Uniform stock is not
  counted. A jersey number is not captured with the uniform.
- The staff forms send no charge lines for an add-on or a uniform (the API takes them). The mobile
  app registers an offering that has choices one offering at a time (the camp week picker is not
  offered for it).
- The `Enrollment` contract does not expose the tier and the day selection; they are on the basket
  line and in the attendance read.
- `GET /v1/uniform-orders` is not paged (one offering or one team entry at a time).

  files above).

- `pnpm verify` as a whole and the staff production build were not run.
- One mobile test outside this track
  (`apps/mobile/src/screens/family-calendar/__tests__/FamilyCalendarScreen.test.tsx`, the team
  fixture label) failed once in eight full runs of the mobile suite because it reads a name

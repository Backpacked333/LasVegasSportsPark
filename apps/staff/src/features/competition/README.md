# Leagues and tournaments, Teams and placement (SCR-S-08 to SCR-S-13, SCR-S-16 to SCR-S-19)

Staff screens for competition setup, fixture generation, readiness, results, disputes and
discipline, standings and brackets, team entries, rosters, placement and youth groups
(`docs/package-a/screen-and-journey-inventory.md` section 5). They talk only to the API through
`@sportspark/api-client`; the API's fake providers stand behind it (§5.2).

Status legend (§6.7): **Verified** = implemented and asserted by a test here (jsdom with a fake
API; not staff acceptance on real devices, §6.3). **Proposed** = engineering proposal awaiting
operator approval, including how an API gap is presented. **Pending decision** = needs an operator
answer; the screen names the decision and its owner instead of inventing the value.

Times are shown in the facility's IANA time zone where the API gives one: a fixture's
`venue.timezone` (fixture page, match sheet, result revisions, and on both dispute screens the
fixture label and the submission, protest-window and decision times) and, on the fixture list,
the time zone of the division's facility from `GET /v1/facilities` (SPK-SCHED-008). Elsewhere
(discipline, appeals, runs) the records name no facility, so the device's time zone is used and
named. Allowed start times of a fixture specification are facility-local `HH:MM` values and are
labelled so.

## API gaps found while building these screens

- No list of the pools of a division (`GET /v1/divisions/{id}/pools`): a pool is reopened by its
  reference.
- No staff read or list of a participant's progression recommendations: only those drafted on the
  page can be shared or withdrawn.
- `ResultRevision`, `Dispute` and `Appeal` carry no team names or facility: the fixture is read once
  per row for its teams and venue time zone.
- No season copy command (SCR-S-08) and no youth group membership (SCR-S-19; DEC-003), both left
  out by stage 3 on purpose.
- No `availableActions` on competition records other than the team workspace, nor on placement or
  capacity records (stage 3 kept the role view in `GET /v1/me/permissions`).
- `GET /v1/seasons/{id}/readiness` filters by time only (`from`, `to`): a page filtered by division
  or fixture state can still exceed the 100-fixture limit, and its rows are then read one by one. A
  `divisionId` (or fixture id list) filter would keep it to one read.
- The OpenAPI summary of `POST /v1/pools/{id}/entries/{teamEntryId}/remove` says "capability
  tournaments", but the service never gates removal (CMP-15 unwinding); the screen follows the
  service and offers removal while the switch is off.

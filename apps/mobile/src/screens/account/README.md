# Account screens (SCR-C-01, SCR-C-02, SCR-C-17, SCR-C-30, SCR-C-31, SCR-C-36, SCR-C-39)

Customer app screens for the account, family access, invitations, preferences and link landing
(spec §2.2, §3.1, §3.18, §4.13, §4.14; journeys A2, A3, C2 in `docs/package-a/journeys.md`; rows
SCR-C-01, SCR-C-02, SCR-C-17, SCR-C-30, SCR-C-31, SCR-C-36 and SCR-C-39 in
`docs/package-a/screen-and-journey-inventory.md`).

Status legend (§6.7): **Verified** = implemented and asserted by a jest test here, against the
fake API of `@sportspark/api-client/testing`. **Proposed** = engineering proposal awaiting
approval. **Pending decision** = waits on an operator answer (owners in
`docs/package-a/decision-register.md`). Nothing here has passed native acceptance on signed builds
and devices (§6.2), including VoiceOver, TalkBack and large text.

the six permissions and date wording; `PersonAccess`, `ClaimForm`, `CommandError`, form controls).

## API observations (reported)

- Fixed in the API during stage-4 integration: `POST /v1/team-invitations/{id}/decline` now
  refuses a stale `expectedRevision` (STALE_REVISION), and `GET /v1/me/team-invitations?status=`
  filters on the effective status like `GET /v1/me/invitations`; the app sends the revision it read
  and still leaves out any row whose `effectiveStatus` is not `pending`.
- Accepting a listed invitation (family or team) and declining a family one need the one-time link
  token; invitations found through `GET /v1/me/invitations` or `/v1/me/team-invitations` cannot be
  accepted in the app without the link until tokens are delivered by messaging (DEC-020).
- `InvitationView` names who accepted (`acceptedByPersonId`) but not who declined, and
  `TeamInvitationView` names neither; a closed invitation can be described as "You accepted" only
  for a family invitation, and a declined one is described without saying who declined it.
- `GrantView` and the invitation views carry no per-record available actions; whether a holder may
  give up a grant comes from the role view of `GET /v1/me/permissions` only.
- A checkout in `/v1/me/registration-checkouts` names no offering or participant, so the pinned
  Account row cannot say which registration it is; the outcome screen does.

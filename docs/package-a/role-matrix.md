## 2. Principals as implemented

| `staffRoles`     | Zero or more `StaffRole` values                                                             | Resolver (the identity module's staff assignments, **Proposed**)                                       |
| `facilityIds`    | Facilities a staff principal may operate in; empty means every facility in the organization | Resolver                                                                                               |
| `sessionId`      | Session reference for audit correlation and revocation                                      | The identity provider token (`sid` claim in the local HS256 provider)                                  |
| `actorLabel`     | Label written into audit snapshots; must never contain child information                    | Resolver                                                                                               |

| Principal kind | Who                                                                | How the kernel authorizes it (**Verified**)                                                                                                           |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `anonymous`    | No bearer token on a route marked `@Public()`                      | Every registry action is denied (`AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED`; test "denies anonymous principals"). Public reads are route-level decisions, not actions. |
| `customer`     | A verified account attached to a person                            | Self (`allowSelf`), relationship grants (`subjectPermission`), team roles (`teamRole`)                                                                |
| `staff`        | An account with one or more staff roles and a facility scope       | Role plus facility scope first; when no listed role is held, the customer paths are evaluated for the staff member's own `personId`                   |
| `system`       | Workers and internal processes (`systemPrincipal(organizationId)`) | Allowed for every action inside its organization, never across organizations (test "system principals are allowed within their organization only")    |

Organization scope is checked before anything else, for every kind (test "never crosses organization scope, even for staff and system actions").

The enum has eleven values. The three remaining §2.1 roles (adult player, parent or guardian, team captain) are deliberately not staff roles: they are customer principals whose authority is a relationship or a grant (§5). A person may hold several enum values at once (`staffRoles` is an array). No role implies another: there is no role hierarchy in the kernel, and `hasStaffRole(principal, ...roles)` is a plain membership test.

Facility scope rule (**Verified**, test "allows staff by role and enforces facility scope"): a target that names a `facilityId` is allowed only when the principal's `facilityIds` is empty (all facilities) or contains that id. A target without a facility (an organization-wide record) is allowed to any facility scope. Which staff member is scoped to which facility depends on the actual facility list: **Pending decision** (owner: Operations, §6.6 "Actual facilities, spaces, subdivisions, hours").

## 5. Customer-side roles are relationships and grants, not labels

SPK-IAM-001 requires these identities to be represented separately; sharing an address, email, surname, or household never establishes authority. SPK-IAM-003 requires each guardian permission to be independent. The kernel implements the evaluation side; the identity and competition modules (scaffolds today) will own the records.

### 5.2 Guardian grants (SPK-IAM-003)

| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

**Verified** evaluation rules (`authorization.service.ts`; tests in `authorization.spec.ts`):

- A grant is checked per subject: for a target naming several participants the actor must hold the grant over every one of them (test "requires authority over every subject in a multi-participant target").
- A person always satisfies a grant check for themselves: `subject === principal.personId` short-circuits before the port is called, so an adult can `collect` or `pay` for themselves without a stored grant (test "allows self when the action permits it").
- A denial on a protected target is reported as `NOT_FOUND`, never `ACCESS_DENIED`, so a stranger cannot learn that a child's record exists (test "reports protected denials as not-found and unprotected denials as access denied").
- The port is called on every decision; nothing is cached in the service, so a revocation takes effect on the next protected request once the identity module evaluates effective dates (SPK-IAM-006).
- Until the identity module registers `RELATIONSHIP_AUTHORITY_IMPL`, the kernel falls back to deny-all with a logged warning (`apps/api/src/kernel/kernel.module.ts`). Today every guardian grant evaluates to false outside tests.

**Proposed** and not yet implemented: invitations recording actor, recipient, scope, requested permissions, expiry, acceptance and revocation (SPK-IAM-004); disputed-access review with temporary restrictions, evidence, ownership, decision history, effective dates and escalation (SPK-IAM-005); revocation affecting exports, private feeds and unsent protected messages (SPK-IAM-006); duplicate review and merge that preserves participation, financial ownership, source identifiers and history without importing unverified permissions (SPK-IAM-007).

## 6. A person may hold several roles; access depends on the action and the scope

§2.1: "A person may hold several roles. Their access depends on the specific action and scope, not merely a role label." The kernel evaluates one action against one target for one principal, in this order (**Verified**, `AuthorizationService.decide`):

1. Unknown action: deny.
2. Anonymous principal: deny (`AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED`).
3. Organization mismatch: deny, for every kind.
4. System principal: allow.
5. Staff principal: if any of the action's `staffRoles` is held and the facility scope matches, allow (basis `staff role`). If a listed role is held but the facility does not match, deny (`facility scope mismatch`). If no listed role is held, continue.
6. `allowSelf` and every subject is the principal's own person: allow (basis `self`).
7. `subjectPermission` and the principal holds that grant (or is the subject) for every subject: allow (basis `relationship:<permission>`).
8. `teamRole` and the target names a `teamEntryId` on which the principal holds that role: allow (basis `team_role:<role>`).
9. Otherwise deny (`no applicable authority`), rendered as `PARTICIPANT_AUTHORITY_REQUIRED` when the target named subjects, `NOT_FOUND` when the target is protected, `ACCESS_DENIED` otherwise.

Worked examples. The mechanics are **Verified**; the actions named are **Proposed** and defined in `permission-matrix.md`.

## 8. Known gaps and ambiguities

1. **Assignment-level scope for coaches and officials.** SPK-UX-007 and SPK-STAFF-004 scope a coach or official to assigned activities with time-bounded access. The kernel's staff path grants by role and facility only. Each workforce, entry, competition, facilities and communications action that a coach or official may perform must load the assignment and compare its person with `principal.personId` before calling `authorization.require`, or the kernel must gain an assignment-scope target field. Such rows are marked ◐ in `permission-matrix.md`. **Proposed**.
2. **Facility mismatch stops guardian evaluation.** In `decide`, a staff principal who holds a listed role but is scoped to a different facility is denied at the staff step; the self, relationship and team paths are never reached. A coach scoped to facility A who is also the parent of a child active at facility B is denied `view` on that child's schedule whenever `coach` is in the action's `staffRoles`. **Verified** behaviour (`authorization.service.ts`, the `roleOk && !facilityOk` branch); not covered by a test (`authorization.spec.ts` tests facility mismatch on a target without subjects). **Proposed** change: fall through to the customer paths on facility mismatch.
3. **One team role per action.** `ActionDefinition.teamRole` is a single value. Captain-or-co-captain actions need either two registered actions or a role hierarchy inside the competition module's `TeamAuthorityPort`. **Verified** limitation; **Pending decision** (owner: League manager).
4. **No "delegate" grant.** The six SPK-IAM-003 permissions do not include the authority to grant or revoke permissions to another adult. **Proposed**: a grant may be created only by a verified guardian and never confers more than the grantor holds. **Pending decision** (owner: Operator/privacy owner).
5. **RSVP permission.** SPK-IAM-003 has no permission for recording a child's availability. **Proposed**: `register` covers `competition.record_availability` for a child. The alternative is a seventh grant. **Pending decision** (owner: Product and operations).
6. **Privileged staff for multifactor authentication (§4.14).** **Proposed** set: `system_administrator`, `finance_operator`, `general_manager`. Enforcement belongs to the managed identity provider. **Pending decision** (owner: Technical lead/account owners).

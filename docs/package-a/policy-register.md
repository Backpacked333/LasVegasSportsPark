# Policy register

Status legend: **Verified** = implemented and tested in this repository today (file cited); **Proposed** = engineering proposal from the specification or from this document, awaiting operator approval; **Pending decision** = needs an operator answer (owner named from spec §6.6). "No answer yet" never becomes "not applicable" (§6.6).

Last derived from specification v2.0 (2026-10-22). Source: `docs/spec/master-specification.md` (repository copy supplied 2026-10-23).

Package A deliverable "Policy register" (§6.5) and the policy half of the "Policy and decision register" (§6.7). The decision half, which this document cites by `DEC-###`, is `docs/package-a/decision-register.md`.

## 1. What this document is

- One row per policy the system must version and enforce: a rule that a command evaluates, that a record cites as its basis, or that a customer acknowledges. 47 policies, `POL-001` to `POL-047` (section 4).
- For each: what it governs, the requirement ids that depend on it, where the specification's entities and interfaces reference it (§4.4 `PolicyVersion`, `Acknowledgment`, `Approval` and the records that carry a policy basis), its status with the §6.6 owner, the specification's proposed default where one exists, and the decision records that answer it.
- A lookup by owner (section 5), so each §6.6 owner can see every policy waiting on them.

**Verified** (by inspection on 2026-10-23): no policy has an approved version. No `PolicyVersion`, `Acknowledgment` or `Approval` table exists in `packages/db/migrations` (only `0001_core_schema.sql` exists), and no file under `docs/` records an operator-approved policy. Every row is therefore **Pending decision**. The "Specification's proposed default" column is the only source of values, and everything in it is **Proposed**.

The "original platform specification" (`.docx`, §1.3) and the "existing local demonstration" (§1.4) are not in this repository. The demonstration's "refund behavior, and competition settings are examples, not approved policies" (§1.4) and are not reproduced here.

## 2. Verified context

What exists in the repository today that a policy will plug into. Each item names the file and, where it is tested, the test.

1. **Commands can cite policies and reasons.** `CommandMetaSchema` in `packages/contracts/src/commands.ts` accepts `policyReferences` (up to 20 strings) and `reason` on every consequential command (§4.9 "Policy references", "Reason for overrides or corrections"). **Verified** for `reason`: `audit_records.reason` is written and read in `apps/api/src/kernel/__tests__/unit-of-work.spec.ts`. `policyReferences` is accepted by the schema but no test exercises it, so it is implemented, not Verified; the reference format is proposed in section 3.
2. **The error contract already names policy outcomes.** `packages/contracts/src/errors.ts` defines `ELIGIBILITY_FAILED`, `MISSING_ACKNOWLEDGMENT`, `CAPACITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `DUPLICATE_ENTITLEMENT`, `QUOTE_EXPIRED`, `REFUND_PENDING` and `PAYMENT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN` with a permitted next action each (§4.10). **Verified** as a contract: `apps/api/src/kernel/__tests__/auth-and-operations.spec.ts` "rejects missing, malformed, expired and unknown tokens with the error contract" proves the `{ code, message, correlationId, nextAction }` shape through `DomainExceptionFilter`. No policy evaluation raises these codes yet.
3. **Lifecycles carry no policy values.** The §4.6 machines in `packages/contracts/src/state-machines.ts` (tested in `state-machines.spec.ts`) define allowed transitions only. A hold has one terminal outcome but no duration; a result can be published but no authority rule is encoded; a refund can be `unknown` but no amount rule exists. The values are the rows below.
4. **Action-specific grants exist.** `GrantPermissions` (`view`, `register`, `pay`, `receive_messages`, `complete_forms`, `collect`) in `apps/api/src/kernel/authorization/actions.ts`; **Verified** by `apps/api/src/kernel/__tests__/authorization.spec.ts` "one permission never implies another: view does not allow collect". The pickup policy (POL-019) decides how `collect` is granted and verified, not whether it is separate.
5. **Append-only records exist for corrections.** The `forbid_mutation` trigger in `packages/db/migrations/0001_core_schema.sql` is applied to `audit_records`; **Verified** by `packages/db/src/core-schema.spec.ts` "audit records are append-only" (as cited in the cross-reference). Policy versions are proposed to use the same trigger (section 3).
6. **Money mechanics exist; the rounding rule is not approved.** `packages/contracts/src/money.ts` rounds basis-point percentages half away from zero and allocates by largest remainder; **Verified** by `money.spec.ts`. These are engineering defaults that POL-012 may confirm or replace (DEC-007).
7. **Ownership of policy versions is a README claim.** `apps/api/src/modules/catalog/README.md` lists "policy versions, acknowledgments" among the catalog module's owned records; the module is a scaffold. That ownership is **Proposed**.
8. **Configuration values that look like policies are not.** `AUTH_TOKEN_TTL_SECONDS` (8 hours), `IDEMPOTENCY_TTL_HOURS` (24) and `OUTBOX_LOCK_LEASE_SECONDS` (60) in `apps/api/src/kernel/config.ts`, and `outbox_events.max_attempts` (10) in `0001_core_schema.sql`, are engineering defaults (**Verified** as code) and appear in no row below.

## 3. How a policy is versioned and enforced (**Proposed**)

This section is an engineering proposal for the §4.4 "Policies" entities. Nothing in it is implemented.

1. **Identity.** Each row below is a policy with a stable `POL-###` id, scoped to an organization (§4.3). A policy may have per-offering, per-competition or per-facility variants; a variant is a `PolicyVersion` whose scope field names the offering, competition or facility, never a new `POL` id.
2. **Versions are immutable.** `PolicyVersion` carries organization, policy id, version number, scope, effective-from and effective-to instants, the rule body, the `Approval` that authorised it, and provenance. Once approved it is append-only (`forbid_mutation`); a change is a new version. An unapproved version may exist for testing only when its body is marked synthetic, and it cannot be cited by a production command.
3. **Approval** records the §6.6 owner role, the named person, the date, and the evidence (questionnaire response, document). The decision register's record (`DEC-###`) is answered in the same change that creates the `Approval`.
4. **Commands cite versions.** `policyReferences` (§4.9) carries `POL-###@<version>` strings. The service resolves each to the effective `PolicyVersion` for the command's reference date (SPK-REG-002 "configured reference date"), evaluates it, and raises the §4.10 code on failure.
5. **Records keep their basis.** A `Quote`, `OrderLine`, `Enrollment`, `Hold`, `WaitlistEntry`, `RefundAttempt`, `Credit`, `Suspension`, `ResultRevision` or `ReleaseRecord` stores the policy version it was evaluated under, so "Historical purchases retain their price and policy basis" (SPK-PAY-002) and later versions never rewrite history (§4.7 invariant 9).
6. **Acknowledgments** link a person (the signer, recorded separately from the staff actor, SPK-REG-009), a `PolicyVersion` with customer-facing wording, and an instant. A command that requires an acknowledgment of a version the person has not signed fails with `MISSING_ACKNOWLEDGMENT`.
7. **Release gate.** A capability is complete only when "Its applicable policies are approved" (§6.8 item 1). The rows below name the capability packages that each policy blocks.

## 7. How this register is maintained (**Proposed**)

1. **Ids are permanent.** `POL-###` is never reused or renumbered. A policy the operator declares not applicable keeps its row with Status "**Pending decision** resolved: not applicable (owner, date, evidence)"; an "unknown" is never rewritten as "not applicable" without those three fields (§6.6).
2. **Status changes only through the decision register.** A row leaves **Pending decision** when every `DEC` record in its "Decision records" column is answered and an approved `PolicyVersion` exists; the same pull request updates both documents and the cross-reference's Status column for the requirement ids listed.
3. **A new policy** is added when a requirement, a command or a record needs a versioned rule that no row covers. The row cites the requirement ids and the `DEC` record that owns the answer; if no `DEC` record exists, one is added first.
4. **Proposed defaults are quoted, not paraphrased into values.** The "Specification's proposed default" column holds only what the specification proposes; an engineering default (section 2, items 6 and 8) is named as such and never promoted into that column.
5. **Code placeholders** for an unapproved policy are marked synthetic and cite the `POL` id, so a test fixture is never mistaken for an approved version (section 3, item 2).
6. **Review trigger.** Every pull request that adds a `policyReferences` value, an eligibility or pricing evaluation, or a table named in section 3 updates the affected rows.
7. **Regeneration check**: `grep -oE 'POL-[0-9]{3}' docs/package-a/policy-register.md | sort -u | wc -l` must equal the row count in section 4 (47), and every `DEC-###` cited here must exist in `docs/package-a/decision-register.md` (`grep -oE 'DEC-[0-9]{3}' docs/package-a/policy-register.md | sort -u` against the same pattern on the decision register).

## 8. Specification ambiguities encountered

Each item is **Proposed** handling.

1. **Cancellation policy has two owners.** §6.6 gives "Refunds, credits, transfers, cash" to Finance and "Waitlist, holds, approvals, basket behavior" to Product and operations, but names no owner for an offering's cancellation terms (SPK-CAT-001). POL-007 lists both.
2. **Quote validity has no §6.6 row.** POL-013 is placed with Finance (prices) and Product and operations (registration acceptance).
3. **Eligibility spans two owners.** Adult competition eligibility (SPK-COMP-002) sits with the League manager and youth eligibility (SPK-YOUTH-001) with the Program manager; POL-001 is one policy with per-scope versions (section 3, item 1) rather than two policies, because SPK-REG-002 evaluates one "eligibility" per registration.
4. **"Pickup" has two meanings** (supervised pickup, POL-019; pickup sessions, POL-045), as in the decision register (ambiguity 2) and the cross-reference (item 2).
5. **Participation consequences of balances** (POL-017) could belong to "Team liability, installments, overpayments" or to "Waitlist, holds, approvals, basket behavior"; the row follows SPK-PAY-006's placement in §3.15 and assigns Finance and league manager.
6. **Visitor categories and arrival guidance** (POL-046) have no §6.6 row; Operations is the closest fit.
7. **The policy-version mechanics in section 3 are not in the specification** beyond the §4.4 entity names and the §4.9 "Policy references" field; they are this document's proposal for how the §6.8 item 1 gate ("Its applicable policies are approved") becomes checkable.

# Delivery plan: from verified code to an accepted pilot

Status legend: **Verified** = implemented and tested in this repository today (file cited); **Proposed** = engineering proposal from the specification or from a document here, awaiting operator approval; **Pending decision** = needs an operator answer (owner named from spec §6.6). "No answer yet" never becomes "not applicable" (§6.6).

Checked against commit `a856709` (`main`, the merge of pull request Backpacked333/LasVegasSportsPark#4) on 2026-11-09, and against the follow-up branch that adds the items marked "closed by the follow-up" below. Every claim cites a file in this repository and, where one applies, a `DEC-*`, `SPK-*`, `POL-*`, `MET-*`, `SPK-AT-*` or ledger row (`L-*`). This document adds no decision and answers none. It names no person, no vendor choice, no date and no price: owners are the §6.6 roles, the named holders are **Pending decision** DEC-017, vendor names appear only as the launch decision packet's **Proposed** values, and sequencing is by dependency, not by calendar.

How to use it: work the phases in order; each phase's exit criterion is a precondition for the next. Do not start Phase 7 (the pilot) until Phases 1 to 6 have produced their evidence rows. The master checklist (section 11) is the sign-off instrument; the launch decision packet ([`launch-decision-packet.md`](launch-decision-packet.md)) is the form the named owners actually sign.

## 2. Phase 0: what "legit" and "deliverable" mean here

### 2.1 Legit

Spec §6.8 (`docs/spec/master-specification.md`): a capability is complete only when all twelve items are met for its scope: (1) applicable policies approved; (2) customer and staff paths work together; (3) permissions enforced server-side; (4) consequential writes safe under concurrency and retry; (5) error and recovery states understandable; (6) financial and operational records reconcile; (7) required follow-up survives interruption; (8) native and staff-device behaviour verified; (9) monitoring and support actions exist; (10) operators complete representative acceptance tasks; (11) evidence identifies the build and environment; (12) release ownership explicit. "Critical defects involving protected data, financial execution, capacity, physical resource conflicts, or supervised release block the affected launch scope."

[`../deploy/production-readiness-checklist.md`](../deploy/production-readiness-checklist.md) applies the twelve items to the Package F pilot scope (DEC-046). At `a856709`: items 3, 4, 5 and 7 met in code, item 11 met as process, items 2, 6 and 9 partially met, items 1, 8, 10 and 12 not met.
